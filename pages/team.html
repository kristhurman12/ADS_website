01 / THIS IS ANDANCESTUDIO

NOT JUST A TEAM.
A COLLECTIVE.

We are an all-hiphop dance team based in Indianapoli, Indiana. Our work starts in the studio and travels to cometition stages around the country and internationally.

The stage is one part of the story. The other is the work we put in together, long before we step into the lights.
